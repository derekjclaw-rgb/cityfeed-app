## Outreach Strategy Recommendations

### Tier 1: Email First (7 businesses)
These have verified emails — send a brief, personalized pitch. Follow up with DM if no reply in 3 days.

### Tier 2: DM Outreach (15+ businesses)
Strong Instagram presence but no public email. Send a brief DM from the City Feed account with a value proposition.

### Tier 3: Phone + Walk-in (50+ businesses)
No email or weak social presence. Best approached by phone first, then walk-in with a one-pager.

### Chinatown Strategy
Most Chinatown businesses are family-owned with limited English-language social media. Recommend:
- **Walk-in with printed materials** (ideally bilingual)
- **Phone during off-peak hours** (2-4 PM typically best)
- **Focus on boba shops first** — younger owners, more social-media savvy, higher Gen Z foot traffic

### Arts District First Friday Opportunity
First Friday events bring massive crowds. Consider:
- Walk-in blitz on a First Friday evening to meet managers/owners in person
- Many bars and shops are at peak staffing, more likely to have a manager available
