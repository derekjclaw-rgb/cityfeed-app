# How You Get Paid — A Host's Guide to City Feed Payouts

Your money is protected at every step. Here's exactly what happens from the moment an advertiser books your space to the moment the money lands in your bank account.

---

## The Short Version

**An advertiser pays upfront. City Feed holds the money safely while the campaign gets set up. The moment you upload your proof photo, your payout is on its way.**

---

## Step by Step

### 1. An advertiser books your space
They pay the full amount upfront — before the campaign ever starts. You'll get an email and a dashboard notification the moment it happens. If they need a booking request approved first, you accept or decline.

### 2. City Feed holds the payment securely
The advertiser's payment sits in a protected holding account. It is not spendable by City Feed and it can't be pulled back by the advertiser once the campaign runs. Think of it like a deposit box that opens only when the work is done.

**Why we do this:** it protects both sides. The advertiser knows their money only moves when their ad is actually up. You know the money is already paid and reserved for you before you lift a finger.

### 3. The campaign gets set up
The advertiser sends over their ad creative (or ships printed materials, depending on the listing). You install or display the ad as booked. Reminder emails keep both sides on track.

### 4. You upload your Proof of Posting
Snap a photo of the ad live in your space and upload it from your booking page. This is the finish line — proof for the advertiser that everything is up and running.

### 5. Your payout releases — immediately
The moment your proof is submitted, City Feed automatically sends your payout. No waiting on manual approval, no invoicing, no chasing anyone down.

**Your payout** = your listing price (plus any print fee) minus City Feed's 7% service fee.

*Example: a 2-day booking at $100/day with a $20 print fee = $220. Minus the 7% fee ($15.40), your payout is $204.60.*

### 6. The money lands in your bank
Your payout arrives in your connected bank account within about 2 business days — that's standard bank processing time, the same as any payment platform. Your dashboard shows the exact date it was sent.

---

## Good to Know

- **Connect your bank early.** Payouts go through your secure Stripe account (the same payment company behind Amazon and Shopify). Set it up once in your dashboard and you're done forever.
- **Every dollar is itemized.** Your dashboard receipt shows the exact math on every booking — daily rate, days, print fee, service fee, payout.
- **Payouts never expire.** If anything ever hiccups (say, your bank connection lapses), the system automatically retries every day until your money goes through.
